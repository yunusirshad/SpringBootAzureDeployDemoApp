Trading plan bot

The plan
  1. Daily: direction. Strong Uptrend or Early Bullish Reversal only. Long only.
  2. Daily / 1h: liquidity. Targets are the liquidity above: prior day high, the 20-hour high, the nearest bearish FVG or order block. If one is closer than 2R, skip: supply is in the way.
  3. 1h: trend. Close above a rising 20 EMA (confluence).
  4. 15m / 1h: setup. Retest: price dips into an unfilled bullish FVG or order block and holds. Fibonacci golden zone: in a trend (a 1h leg of 2 ATR or more), drawn swing low to swing high, price pulls back into 0.5–0.618; a close below 0.786 kills the setup and the stop goes under 0.786. Reversal: a 15m candle sweeps the recent low (or the prior day low) and closes back above it, then price closes above that candle's high. Breakout: a 15m close through the prior day high on 1.5× volume.
  5. 5m: confirmation and entry. A bullish 5m close above its 20 EMA and the previous high. Enter at the next open; stop under the zone or the 5m swing low (3% at most); target the first liquidity at 2R or more.
  6. Magnet zones. A magnet zone above the entry is a target and, closer than 2R, a reason to skip; a stop never sits inside a zone (it moves under it); an entry at a zone earns a grade point.
  7. Management and exit. A swing trade held up to the set number of trading days (5 by default; 1 makes it a day trade). Once up 1R, the stop moves to the entry (breakeven). Exits: the stop, the target, or 3:45 PM on the last day of the hold.
  8. High probability only. Setups are graded A/B/C by confluence (1h trend, 1h zone, FVG + OB overlap, Fibonacci golden zone, SPY with you, volume, R:R 3+). The plan is backtested on every screened stock over the last 60 days of 5-minute data, and a BUY NOW is sent only for a grade that has won at least the minimum win rate over at least the minimum number of trades.

API guide

GEX levels

Every listed stock, from its option chain, read once a day. Open interest is summed by strike over the expiries in the next 45 days. Call wall and put wall are the strikes with the most call and put open interest; King is the strike with the largest net gamma. Each level shows its open interest and its share of the total. Expiry is the date holding the most open interest. It is not a forecast.

PV Confirm Signals

Nested under the trend check (uptrend = SMA50 above SMA200, unchanged). Daily bars, long only. In an uptrend the four PV Confirm rules are: close above SMA200, SPY above its SMA200, RSI(2) under 10, and volume under its 50-day average. All four on a completed daily bar is a BUY at the next open, with the stop at entry − 2.5 × ATR(14). A trade exits on the first profitable close (TAKE PROFIT), a touch of the stop (STOP) or after 10 bars (TIME EXIT). Today's bar counts from 4:15 PM ET, when the alerts go out.

Quantum Shift backtest

Quantum Shift scores the jump from a coiled, quiet tape to a new trend: coil (30), break past the 20 EMA in ATRs (30), momentum z-score (25) and volume (15). A shift fires when energy clears the threshold and the close is outside the 20 EMA ± 1 ATR. Trades enter at the next bar's open, stop 2 ATR, trail 2.5 ATR off the best close, and exit on an opposite shift or after the bar limit. Costs are charged on both sides.

Session phases

Every Asia (6 PM–2 AM ET), London (2–8 AM ET) and New York (8 AM–5 PM ET) session is scored for accumulation, manipulation, absorption and distribution, each 0–100%. The scores come from 5-minute candles whatever timeframe you pick, so the boxes and percentages never change when you switch. Works on stocks, futures (ES=F), forex (EURUSD=X) and crypto (BTC-USD). Stocks have no Asia session and only a thin London one.
